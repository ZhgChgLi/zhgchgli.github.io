#### Preface

A spontaneous birthday trip with friends in the summer of 2026; the destination — Okinawa, a place none of us had been to before. Although we had visited Japan many times, we never went to Okinawa mainly because public transportation is inconvenient. Without self-driving, it's almost impossible to explore Okinawa. Also, Okinawa's culture feels more diverse and less traditionally Japanese.
